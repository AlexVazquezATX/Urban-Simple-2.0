# Manager training guide

For Urban Simple night managers. It covers the full loop: how work gets
scheduled, what the crew reports, how you review, and how clients stay
informed. Plan on about an hour the first time, with the app open next to you.

## 1. The loop in one picture

```
Associate (My Night)                 Manager (you)                      Client
──────────────────                  ──────────────                     ──────
Clock in at the stop
Tap only what wasn't done + why ─► Alert: "Unfinished work"  ─► Heads-up email/text
Report a problem ────────────────► Alert: issue (internal)            (only contacts who opted in)
Finish & clock out
                                    Nightly review of the location
                                    Pain points become internal issues
                                    Resolve / reply / share ─────────► Update + resolution email
                                                                      ◄── Client reports, requests,
                                                                          replies, visit ratings
```

Everything lands in **Operations → Issues**, and the bell at the top shows your
alerts. Email always goes out too. Texts go out only if you turn on "Text me
alerts" in your profile.

## 2. Your night

1. **Dashboard** shows tonight's board: every associate stop, who is clocked in,
   how many items are done, and anything flagged not done. **Needs you** lists
   what is waiting on you: unfinished work, client reports and requests, low
   ratings, and anyone still clocked in after 10 hours.
2. **Nightly Reviews** is your route. Open a location to review it.
   - The **Associate report** at the top shows what the crew did: hours, items
     done, and anything marked not done with the reason, notes and photos.
     Items the associate marked not done start out as "not done" in your form.
   - Rate each item, add photos, log pain points, and submit.
   - Pain points become **internal** issues. The client doesn't see them unless
     you share them.
3. You get alerted, without asking, when:
   - an associate finishes a stop with items not done,
   - an associate reports a problem,
   - a client reports an issue, sends a request, replies, or rates a visit 3 stars or lower,
   - an associate hasn't clocked in 45 minutes after the shift starts (only for
     associates who already use the app),
   - someone has been clocked in more than 10 hours.

## 3. Working an issue

Open it from the bell, the dashboard, or **Operations → Issues** (tabs:
Unfinished work, Client reports, Requests, Internal).

- **Assign** it to whoever will handle it.
- **Reply.** Replies go to the client by email. Tick **Internal note** to keep a
  comment between staff.
- **Send to client** on internal issues when the client should know. Add a short
  message. This also makes the issue visible in their portal.
- **Resolve.** Write the resolution in plain words ("Re-mopped the walk-in at 6am
  and restocked degreaser"). The client gets it in the "Resolved" email, so write
  it for them.
- If the client replies "not fixed", the issue reopens on its own and you're alerted.

## 4. Client alerts: who hears what

Each client has contacts. On the client's **Contacts** tab, each contact has:

- **Email alerts** and/or **Text alerts** (text needs their consent, so ask first).
- **Alert locations**: which locations they care about. Leave it empty for all
  of the client's locations.

For example, at Horseshoe Bay the Yacht Club lead gets alerts for Yacht Club and
YC Pool Bar only, and the F&B director gets all of them.

Contacts with alerts on get:
- a heads-up as soon as an associate finishes with items not done, so their own
  team can cover it before opening,
- anything you **Send to client**,
- replies and resolutions on issues at their locations.

Check a location's **Alerts** card to see exactly who will hear about it. If
someone replies STOP to a text, they're opted out automatically and text alerts
can't be turned back on for them from our side.

## 5. Setting up a location (with an admin)

Open **Operations → Launch Setup**. A location is ready when every column is green:

1. **Checklist** assigned (build it from the library; items come with Spanish).
2. **Crew** assigned, with **nights and hours** (this puts shifts on the calendar).
3. **Manager**: you, set as the default manager in the location's dispatch profile.
4. **Client alerts**: at least one contact opted in.
5. **Next 7 days** shows shifts. Use **Fill crew shifts** if not.

## 6. Training your crew

Hand them `docs/training/associate-guide.md` (English and Spanish, one page).
Walk the first night with each new associate:

1. Sign in on their phone and add it to the home screen (the app shows how).
2. Tap **I'm here** at the first stop. That clocks them in.
3. Explain that everything starts as done: they only tap **Not done** + a
   reason for what they couldn't finish, and **Report a problem** for anything
   off the list. It saves by itself.
4. **All done · Clock out**, tap again. Three taps on a normal stop.
5. Tell them why "not done" matters: it isn't a strike against them, it's how
   the client hears about it before opening.

## 7. Quick answers

- **An associate finished by mistake.** Visits lock when finished. Fix the
  record from the issue or the review, and note it.
- **A stop isn't on someone's My Night.** Their assignment is missing nights or
  hours, or no shift was generated. Check Launch Setup, then Fill crew shifts.
- **A client says they didn't get an alert.** Check the location's Alerts card,
  then the contact's alert settings and locations.
