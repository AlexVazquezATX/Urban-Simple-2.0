# Your Urban Simple portal

A quick guide for our clients. The portal is where you see what we did each
night, tell us about anything we missed, ask for something extra, and hear back
from us. It works on your phone.

**Sign in:** urbansimple.net/portal. Your invite email has a link to set your
password. If you lose it, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page.

## What you can do

**See every visit.** The **Cleaning log** lists each night we serviced your
locations, with our manager's review and photos when they were taken.

**Rate a visit.** On the cleaning log, tap the stars on any visit from the last
30 days and add a comment if you like. Low ratings go straight to your manager.

**Hear about anything not finished.** If our crew can't finish something (a room
was locked, staff were still working, equipment was down), we can let you know
that night, before you open, so your team can plan around it. Turn this on
under **Alerts**.

**Report a problem.** Go to **Issues**, then **Report something**. Pick the
location, describe it, and add photos. Your manager is notified right away and
you get a confirmation email.

**Ask for something extra.** Go to **Requests**, then **New request**, for deep cleans, restocks, schedule
changes, skipping a night, or event prep. Add a preferred date if you have one.

**Talk it through.** Every issue and request has a conversation. Reply in the
portal and we reply back. You get an email each time we respond, start working
on it, or resolve it. If something isn't fixed, reply and it reopens.

**Walkthroughs.** Use **Walkthrough** to capture your own zone-by-zone check with
photos. Any zone you mark as an issue comes to us automatically.

**Documents.** Your service agreement, certificates of insurance and other
paperwork live under **Documents**. You can add your own files too.

**Your team.** Invite coworkers under **Team** so they can use the portal too.

## Alerts

Under **Alerts** you choose:

- **Email alerts**: unfinished work, updates and resolutions.
- **Text alerts**: the same heads-up by text. Message frequency varies. Msg and
  data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
- **Which locations** you want to hear about (for example, only your outlet).

Questions? Reply to any email from us, or write to hello@urbansimple.net.
